---
name: RunBuddy profile photos
description: Product scope and visibility rules for runner profile photos.
---

RunBuddy profile-photo editing is required on both the website and mobile app. Photos are accessible to anyone who has the image URL; the product must not imply that uploaded photos are private or access-controlled.

**Why:** The product owner chose both platforms. The current storage-serving design intentionally supports link-based access, so the visibility disclosure is important when users select or change a photo.

**How to apply:** Preserve web/mobile parity for avatar editing and keep a clear visibility notice anywhere users upload or change profile photos.
