---
name: RunBuddy-first product direction
description: Product hierarchy decision for the RunMatch community app.
---

RunBuddy is the main entry point and should receive the strongest homepage, navigation, and mobile emphasis. RunDate remains available as a secondary experience rather than the default identity.

**Why:** The product should foreground finding running companions for local and travel runs, instead of presenting dating as the primary use case.

**How to apply:** Future homepage, navigation, onboarding, copy, and promotional changes should lead with RunBuddy unless the user explicitly asks for a RunDate-focused experience.
